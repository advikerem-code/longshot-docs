Long Shot uses 3D models, sounds and music made by other artists. The works on this page are shared under Creative Commons Attribution licenses (CC BY), which ask us to name each artist. Thank you to all of them.

We converted some models for the game and reduced their detail. The cities, cars and birds appear in the opening film. Some textures were edited, as noted below.
