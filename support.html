Frequently asked questions

My Remove Ads purchase is missing.
Open the game and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple account on iPhone and to your Google account on Android, so sign in with the account you used to buy.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?
Yes. The game has no account or cloud save. Your progress, your coins and any unlimited ammo you have left are saved only on your phone, and deleting the game deletes them. Remove Ads and other purchases that do not run out can be restored with Restore purchases.

How do I change my ad choices?
In the game's Settings. On iPhone, you can also use Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can also delete your advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads (on some phones Settings > Privacy > Ads).

How do I delete my data?
The game has no account, so there is no account to delete. Deleting the game deletes the save on your phone. Game measurement data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days. Details: Privacy Policy, section 7.

Can I get a refund?
On iPhone, refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, Google Play handles refunds under its own rules: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. You can also write to us if something goes wrong.

Who made the models and sounds?
Many artists. Their names are on the Credits page.
