Frequently asked questions

My purchase is missing.
Tap Restore purchases. You can find it at the top of the store and on the Privacy tab of Settings. Purchases are tied to your Apple account on iPhone and to your Google account on Android, so sign in with the account you used to buy. Remove Ads and rifles bought with money come back this way. Coins you bought are kept in your save, not in your Apple or Google account.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones or delete the game?
Your progress is backed up to the cloud. To bring it to a new iPhone, turn on Game Center before you switch, and open the game once. Then use the same Game Center account on the new iPhone. Moving progress to a new Android phone is not available yet, and progress does not move between iPhone and Android. Without Game Center, if you delete and reinstall the game on the same iPhone, you will usually continue where you left off. On Android, this depends on your phone's Google backup.

Can I play without an internet connection?
Yes. Your progress is saved on your phone and goes to the cloud the next time you are online. Ads and purchases need a connection.

How do I change my ad choices?
On the Privacy tab of the game's Settings. On iPhone, you can also use Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can also delete your advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads (on some phones Settings > Privacy > Ads).

How do I delete my account and data?
Open Settings in the game, go to the Account tab, choose Delete account and data and confirm. This deletes your cloud save and your player account, and resets the game on your phone. It cannot be undone. If you cannot open the game, write to us and we will delete your data within 30 days. Details: Privacy Policy, section 7.

Can I get a refund?
On iPhone, refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, Google Play handles refunds under its own rules: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. You can also write to us if something goes wrong.

Many artists. Their names are on the Credits page.
