Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Long Shot game ("the game") collects and why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you can use your rights. For players in Türkiye, this policy is also the information notice under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

In short

  • When you first open the game, a player account is created for you automatically. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone and backed up to the cloud. If you use Game Center on iPhone, your progress comes back on a new phone.
  • We measure in-game events and errors to improve the game (for example, starting and finishing a mission).
  • The game contains ads (Google AdMob). Your advertising identifier is used for personalized ads only if you allow it. The game asks at the end of your first mission (see 2.6).
  • Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach us.
  • We do not sell your data. You can delete your account and your data from inside the game (see section 7).

2. Data we collect and why

2.1 Automatic player account (Firebase Authentication)

When you first open the game, an anonymous account is created for you in Google's Firebase Authentication service. Your account is identified by a random code (player ID). Firebase keeps the time the account was created and last signed in, and keeps the IP address used for sign-in for a few weeks for security. On iPhone, the account key is stored in the keychain, so if you delete and reinstall the game you will usually get the same account back. On Android, the key is stored in the game's data on your phone. Deleting the game deletes the key too, and if your phone's Google backup is on, it may come back when you reinstall.

Purpose: to back up your progress to the cloud and to link measurement data to the same player.

Game Center. If you use Game Center on your iPhone, the game links your player account to it. This is so your progress, coins and rifles come back when you move to a new phone or reinstall the game. When linked, Firebase Authentication stores the player ID that Game Center gives this game and your nickname there, together with your player account. We do not receive your email address, real name or friends list from Apple. The game has no leaderboard, and your nickname is not shown to other players. If you do not want the link, keep Game Center off. The game works the same with the automatic account, but your progress cannot move to a new phone. If on a new phone the link finds your earlier account, the game switches to it: the progress from both phones is merged, and the temporary account created on the new phone is deleted together with its cloud save. On Android, linking with Google Play Games is not available yet.

2.2 Cloud save (Cloud Firestore)

Your save is kept on a cloud page linked to your player ID:

  • your progress: the missions you have finished, your stars and personal records in each one (such as score, time, accuracy and longest shot), the number of attempts and wins, and the areas and cities you have finished,
  • your coins, and the rifles, upgrades, scopes and other items you own and have selected,
  • your purchase status: whether you have Remove Ads, when your unlimited ammo ends, and the App Store or Google Play transaction IDs of purchases already delivered (so the same purchase is never delivered twice),
  • which one-time free offers you have used, and how many coin ads you have watched today,
  • your settings and your answers in the permission windows, so a new phone does not ask again,
  • lifetime totals such as shots, hits, coins earned and spent, ads watched and purchases, and when you first opened the game,
  • whether your account is linked to Game Center, the game version, the device type (iOS or Android), when the save was last updated, and technical counters used to merge the save on your phone with the cloud save.

Single shots are not kept in the cloud save. Only your own account can read and write this page. The server is located in Germany (Frankfurt).

Purpose: so that you do not lose your progress, to deliver your purchases correctly, and to see your progress when you write to us for help.

2.3 Game measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We measure in-game events to improve the game, balance its difficulty and understand how it is played. Examples:

  • opening the game and playing the tutorial,
  • starting, finishing, failing, quitting or retrying a mission, and the counts for that attempt (such as stars, time, shots, hits and accuracy),
  • running out of ammo or time, and how you carried on,
  • earning coins,
  • store and purchase events (product, price, currency),
  • ad requests, ad impressions, rewards and the estimated revenue from ads,
  • your answers in the permission windows (see 2.6), and whether your account is linked to Game Center,
  • error messages caught in the game.

In addition, Firebase automatically collects: an app instance ID, session and usage time, device model, operating system version, language, game version, approximate location derived from your IP address (country, region and city level) and App Store or Google Play purchase events. Firebase may also collect your advertising identifier: on iPhone (IDFA) only if you allowed tracking in Apple's prompt, and on Android.

Measurement data is linked to the random app instance ID, not to your player ID, so it is not matched with your cloud save. Google signals is turned off, so this data is not linked to your Google account. We do not know your name or email address, so it is not linked to those either. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, measurement follows your choice in Google's consent form (see 2.6).

Purpose: to improve the game and its balance, fix bugs, and measure how many people play and how often they come back.

2.4 Error reports

The game does not use a separate crash reporting service. If an error happens in the game, the error message is sent as a measurement event (see 2.3). If the game crashes and you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may also share a crash report with us. These reports do not identify you.

Purpose: to find and fix errors.

2.5 Remote settings and tests (Firebase Remote Config and A/B Testing)

When it opens, the game downloads some of its settings from Firebase Remote Config, for example rewards, prices and difficulty. This lets us adjust these numbers without releasing a new version. New values apply the next time you open the game, never in the middle of a mission.

With Firebase A/B Testing, we try different settings with groups of players, for example the strength of aim assist, the amount of ammo or a price in the store. Your group is chosen at random. For this, Firebase uses the installation ID on your phone (Firebase Installations), the game version, the platform, your language and your country. This information is not used to identify you or for ads.

2.6 Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and contains ads: optional rewarded ads (for example, for more ammo, more time or more coins) and interstitial ads shown now and then after a mission. There are no banner ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent abuse, AdMob may collect: your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device identifiers, the ads you have seen and your interactions with them, interactions with the app (for example, opening the app), and error and performance data.

The game asks for your ad choices at the end of your first mission, before it shows any ad.

  • iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency): before Apple's prompt, the game explains why it asks. If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you more relevant (personalized) ads and to measure ads. If you do not allow it, your advertising identifier is not used. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. The game works the same either way, and no reward depends on your answer. You can change your choice at any time on your iPhone under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks whether you allow your advertising identifier to be used for personalized ads. If you do not allow it, or have not chosen yet, ads are requested without personalization. You can change your choice at any time on the Privacy tab of the game's Settings. You can also delete your advertising identifier in your phone's settings (on most phones Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). After you delete it, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized with your advertising identifier.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks whether you allow your data to be used for advertising and measurement. You can change your choice at any time on the Privacy tab of the game's Settings.
  • On iPhone, ad networks may use Apple's SKAdNetwork to measure how many downloads ads bring, without using an identifier that identifies you.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.7 In-app purchases (App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat)

The game offers optional purchases, for example Remove Ads (removes interstitial ads; rewarded ads stay optional), unlimited ammo, coin packs and rifles that are sold only for money. Apple takes the payment on iPhone and Google Play on Android. Your card and payment details never reach us or RevenueCat.

We use RevenueCat to verify, deliver and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes: your player ID, the product purchased, the transaction ID, price, currency, store country, purchase time, and device and app version information. Purchase events are also sent to measurement (see 2.3).

2.8 Data stored on your phone

  • Your save is also stored on your phone: your progress and stars, your coins, the items you own and have selected, your settings, your permission choices and the transaction IDs of purchases already delivered. The game can be played without an internet connection.
  • The key of your automatic account is stored in the keychain on iPhone and in the game's data on Android.
  • Google (Firebase, AdMob and the consent form) and RevenueCat keep small records on your phone for their own operation (for example, the app instance ID, your ad consent choice and a cache of purchase information).

Deleting the game deletes the save on your phone, but it does not delete your cloud save or your account. On iPhone, the account key in the keychain may remain. If your phone backs up app data (for example to iCloud or Google), the backup may include the game's save. Apple and Google handle these backups. To delete everything, see section 7.

2.9 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location (GPS), contacts, photos, or camera or microphone recordings. The game does not ask for your age, gender or date of birth, and it does not send notifications. Your payment card details never reach us. If you turn on gyro aiming in Settings, the game reads your phone's motion sensor while you aim; this stays on your phone. If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to answer you.

7. Deleting your data and withdrawing your permissions

  • In the game: open Settings, go to the Account tab, choose Delete account and data and confirm. This deletes your cloud save and your automatic account (together with its Game Center link) and resets the save on your phone. Measurement data is not linked to your account; it is deleted automatically (see section 6). It needs an internet connection and cannot be undone: your progress, stars, coins and rifles will be lost. Remove Ads and rifles you paid for are tied to your Apple account on iPhone and to your Google account on Android, and they come back with Restore purchases. Coins you bought do not come back. Your Game Center account itself is not deleted; it belongs to Apple.
  • By email: write to advikerem@gmail.com. To confirm that the account is yours, we may ask you a few questions. We will complete your request within 30 days at the latest and let you know the result.
  • Deleting the game does not delete everything (see 2.8).
  • Purchase records may be kept at RevenueCat for our legal obligations and so that you can restore your purchases. Write to us if you want them deleted.
  • Ad permission: on the Privacy tab of the game's Settings. On iPhone, also Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can also delete your advertising identifier under Settings > Google > Ads (on some phones Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Objecting to measurement: you can object to game measurement by writing to advikerem@gmail.com.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it is; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; to request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate; to request its deletion or destruction; to request that such correction or deletion be notified to the third parties to whom it was transferred; to object to a result against you that arises solely from analysis by automated systems; and to claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time.

If you live in certain US states (for example, California), you have the right to know about and delete your data, and to opt out of its sharing for personalized advertising. To opt out, turn off tracking on your iPhone; on Android, turn off personalized ads on the Privacy tab of the game's Settings or delete your advertising identifier; or write to us.

To use your rights, write to advikerem@gmail.com. You can also apply under the KVKK through the methods listed in the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. We will handle your request free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board in Türkiye (https://www.kvkk.gov.tr) or, in Europe, to the data protection authority of the country where you live.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and their data has been collected, write to advikerem@gmail.com and we will delete that data.

10. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Only your own account can read and write your cloud save, and this rule is enforced on the server. We do not run servers of our own; the data in this policy is processed by the service providers listed in section 4. No system is completely secure. If a data breach happens, we will report it as required by law.
