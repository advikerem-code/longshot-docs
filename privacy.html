Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Long Shot game ("the game") collects and why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you can use your rights. For players in Türkiye, this policy is also the information notice under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

In short

  • The game has no player accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved only on your phone. There is no cloud save.
  • We measure in-game events and crashes to improve the game (for example, starting and finishing a mission).
  • The game contains ads (Google AdMob). Your advertising identifier is used for personalized ads only if you allow it. The game asks at the end of your first mission (see 2.4).
  • Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach us.
  • We do not sell your data.

  • Contact: advikerem@gmail.com

Write to this address with any question or request about this policy or your data.

2. Data we collect and why

2.1 Game measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We measure in-game events to improve the game, balance its difficulty and understand how it is played. Examples:

  • opening the game and moving between screens,
  • starting, finishing, failing, quitting or retrying a mission, and the counts for that attempt (such as stars, time, shots, hits and accuracy),
  • running out of ammo and how it was refilled,
  • earning and spending coins, and the rifles, scopes and other items you choose,
  • changes to your settings,
  • store and purchase events (product, price, currency),
  • ad requests, ad impressions, rewards and the estimated revenue from ads,
  • your answers in the permission windows (see 2.4),
  • error messages caught in the game.

We also send player properties: the highest mission you have reached, your total stars, your selected rifle and scope, whether aim assist is on, whether you have Remove Ads, whether unlimited ammo is active, whether you have made a purchase with real money and which test group you are in (see 2.3).

In addition, Firebase automatically collects: an app instance ID, session and usage time, device model, operating system version, language, game version, approximate location derived from your IP address (country, region and city level) and App Store or Google Play purchase events. Firebase may also collect your advertising identifier: on iPhone (IDFA) only if you allowed tracking in Apple's prompt, and on Android.

Google signals is turned off, so this data is not linked to your Google account. The game has no player accounts, so this data is not linked to your name or email address either. A copy of these events may be exported to Google BigQuery for detailed analysis.

Purpose: to improve the game and its balance, fix bugs, and measure how many people play and how often they come back.

2.2 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, Crashlytics sends us a crash report. It contains where in the game's code the crash happened, the device model, operating system version, game version, the amount of memory and disk space, the time of the crash, a random installation ID and the last in-game events before the crash.

Purpose: to find and fix crashes.

2.3 Remote settings and tests (Firebase Remote Config and A/B Testing)

When it opens, the game downloads some of its settings from Firebase Remote Config, for example rewards, prices and difficulty. This lets us adjust these numbers without releasing a new version. New values apply the next time you open the game, never in the middle of a mission.

With Firebase A/B Testing, we try different settings with groups of players, for example the strength of aim assist, the amount of ammo or a price in the store. Your group is chosen at random. For this, Firebase uses the installation ID on your phone (Firebase Installations), the game version, the platform, your language and your country. This information is not used to identify you or for ads.

2.4 Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and contains ads: optional rewarded ads (for example, for more ammo, more time or a bigger reward at the end of a mission) and interstitial ads shown occasionally after a mission. There are no banner ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent abuse, AdMob may collect: your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device identifiers, the ads you have seen and your interactions with them, interactions with the app (for example, opening the app), and error and performance data.

The game asks for your ad choices at the end of your first mission, before it shows any ad.

  • iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency): before Apple's prompt, the game explains why it asks. If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you more relevant (personalized) ads and to measure ads. If you do not allow it, your advertising identifier is not used. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. The game works the same either way, and no reward depends on your answer. You can change your choice at any time on your iPhone under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks whether you allow your advertising identifier to be used for personalized ads. If you do not allow it, or have not chosen yet, ads are requested without personalization. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings. You can also delete your advertising identifier in your phone's settings (on most phones Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). After you delete it, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized with your advertising identifier.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks whether you allow your data to be used for advertising. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings.
  • On iPhone, ad networks may use Apple's SKAdNetwork to measure how many downloads ads bring, without using an identifier that identifies you.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.5 In-app purchases (App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat)

The game offers optional purchases, for example Remove Ads (removes interstitial ads; rewarded ads stay optional), unlimited ammo and cosmetic packs. Apple takes the payment on iPhone and Google Play on Android. Your card and payment details never reach us or RevenueCat.

We use RevenueCat to verify, deliver and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes: a random RevenueCat ID, the product purchased, the transaction ID, price, currency, store country, purchase time, and device and app version information. Purchase events are also sent to measurement (see 2.1).

2.6 Data stored on your phone

  • Your save stays on your phone: your progress and stars, your coins, the items you own and have selected, your settings, your permission choices and the store transaction IDs of purchases already delivered (so the same purchase is never delivered twice). We do not receive your save.
  • Google (Firebase, AdMob and the consent form) and RevenueCat keep small records on your phone for their own operation (for example, the app instance ID, your ad consent choice and a cache of purchase information).

Deleting the game deletes its save on your phone, including your coins and any unlimited ammo you have left. Purchases that do not run out, such as Remove Ads, can be restored. If your phone backs up app data (for example to iCloud or Google), the backup may include the game's save. Apple and Google handle these backups.

2.7 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location (GPS), contacts, photos, or camera or microphone recordings. The game does not ask for your age, gender or date of birth. Your payment card details never reach us. If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to answer you.

7. Deleting your data and withdrawing your permissions

  • Deleting the game deletes the save on your phone (see 2.6). The installation and app instance IDs on your phone are reset too, so data from a new installation is not linked to the old data.
  • Measurement and crash data are not linked to your name or email address, and the game has no account we could look you up by. This data is deleted automatically (see section 6). If you have a request about it, write to advikerem@gmail.com.
  • Purchase records may be kept at RevenueCat for our legal obligations and so that you can restore your purchases. Write to us if you want them deleted.
  • Ad permission: in the game's Settings. On iPhone, also Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can also delete your advertising identifier under Settings > Google > Ads (on some phones Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Objecting to measurement: you can object to game measurement by writing to advikerem@gmail.com.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it is; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; to request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate; to request its deletion or destruction; to request that such correction or deletion be notified to the third parties to whom it was transferred; to object to a result against you that arises solely from analysis by automated systems; and to claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time.

If you live in certain US states (for example, California), you have the right to know about and delete your data, and to opt out of its sharing for personalized advertising. To opt out, turn off tracking on your iPhone; on Android, turn off the permission in the game's Settings or delete your advertising identifier; or write to us.

To use your rights, write to advikerem@gmail.com. You can also apply under the KVKK through the methods listed in the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. We will handle your request free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board in Türkiye (https://www.kvkk.gov.tr) or, in Europe, to the data protection authority of the country where you live.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and their data has been collected, write to advikerem@gmail.com and we will delete that data.

10. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not run our own servers; the data in this policy is processed by the service providers listed in section 4. No system is completely secure. If a data breach happens, we will report it as required by law.
